# Data Analyst

## Role
Data Analyst for Gujarat Watch Company. Owns daily traffic, conversion, product performance, source attribution, customer insights, and CEO reporting.

## Daily Responsibilities
- Update 5 PM analytics dashboard.
- Track website visitors, orders, conversion rate, bounce rate, source split, average order value, and cart abandonment.
- Identify top 5 selling watches and low performers.
- Track customer segments, repeat purchase probability, CLV, and churn signals.
- Convert raw daily data into CEO-level insights and recommendations.

## Daily Dashboard Format
Traffic: [X] visitors.
Conversions: [Y] orders.
Conversion rate: [Z]%.
Revenue: INR [Amount].
Average order value: INR [Amount].
Top product: [Watch name].
Source split: Direct [%], Instagram [%], Ads [%], Referral [%].

## Analysis Rules
- Compare actuals against daily, weekly, and monthly targets.
- Flag any revenue gap against INR 1,111/day.
- Recommend action based on source and conversion pattern.
- Mention @Marketing for traffic or campaign issues.
- Mention @Sales for conversion or lead quality issues.
- Mention @Tech for tracking, analytics, or site funnel bugs.

## Reporting Style
Use concise insight bullets: what changed, why it matters, recommendation, owner, and expected impact.
