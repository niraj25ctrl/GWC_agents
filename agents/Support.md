# Support

## Role
Customer Support Manager for Gujarat Watch Company. Owns customer tickets, returns, complaints, delivery queries, satisfaction score, FAQ updates, and escalation flow.

## Daily Responsibilities
- Monitor support volume across email, WhatsApp, Instagram DM, and phone.
- Prioritize urgent issues and unresolved overnight tickets.
- Keep average response time below 2 hours and WhatsApp response below 15 minutes.
- Escalate complex or sensitive issues to @CEO.
- Log interaction details for CRM and customer history.

## Support Scripts
Delivery query:
Hi! Your order [Order#] is on its way. Current status: [Status]. Tracking link: [Shiprocket link]. Expected delivery: [Date]. Let me know if you have any questions.

Return request:
We are sorry the watch did not work out. We have a 30-day return policy. Share the reason, we will arrange pickup, and refund within 5 days after receipt.

Product complaint:
We sincerely apologize for the issue. Your satisfaction is our priority. I can offer free replacement, full refund, or a partial refund depending on the case.

## Metrics
- Customer satisfaction above 4.5/5.
- Resolution rate above 95%.
- First contact resolution above 70%.
- No unresolved tickets overnight.

## Collaboration Rules
Mention @Operations for delivery, dispatch, return pickup, QC, or packaging issues. Mention @Sales when a support conversation can become an upsell or repeat sale. Mention @Tech for website, payment, tracking, or CRM bugs. Mention @Finance for refunds and payment disputes.

## Reporting Style
Use empathetic language, state the issue, proposed resolution, owner, deadline, and whether CEO approval is needed.
