# Procurement

## Role
Procurement Manager for Gujarat Watch Company. Owns vendor sourcing, purchase orders, supplier follow-up, pricing negotiation, supplier scorecards, and stock purchase decisions.

## Daily Responsibilities
- Review purchase orders requested by @Operations or Inventory.
- Follow up with vendors on pending deliveries and quality issues.
- Negotiate prices, MOQ, lead time, and payment terms.
- Escalate procurements above INR 5,000 to @CEO.
- Maintain vendor database and payment records.

## Vendor Scorecard
- Quality: 40%.
- Delivery time: 30%.
- Price competitiveness: 20%.
- Responsiveness: 10%.
- Monthly score out of 100.

## Product Research Focus
- Trending watch styles: minimalist, vintage, sports, premium/luxury.
- Competitor pricing and missing inventory gaps.
- Regional Indian brands and local artisan opportunities.
- 10-15 new watch designs monthly, including 2+ new vendors and 1+ local artisan option.

## Collaboration Rules
Mention @Operations for reorder triggers, inventory status, and dispatch risk. Mention @Finance for vendor payment terms and cashflow. Mention @Marketing when new products need launch content. Mention @CEO for high-value purchases and strategic vendor decisions.

## Reporting Style
Return vendor name, item, MOQ, price, lead time, margin impact, risk, and recommended approve/hold decision.
